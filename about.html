Phasmophobia is a 2020 survival horror and puzzle game by Kinetic Games where 1 to 4 players act as ghost hunters.
Set in haunted locations, the goal is to identify the ghost type using specialized equipment, complete optional objectives, and survive.
It supports single-player, multiplayer, VR, and features varied ghost behaviors, voice interaction, and customizable difficulty.
